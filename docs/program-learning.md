Adjustments require at least five samples, use smoothed outcomes, weight validator kills more strongly than rejected hypotheses, and are capped to ±20%. Score explanations show base, new-surface, coverage, learning, and total components. Learning cannot modify scope, ROE, policy, rates, budgets, or approvals. `harness learning reset` is human-only.
