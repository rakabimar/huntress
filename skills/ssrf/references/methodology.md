# SSRF pipeline methodology

Establish server-side fetching with a unique marker at an authorized fixture. Attribute source and timing. From implementation/recon evidence, choose one parser, normalization, DNS, redirect, or forwarding assumption and change only that component. Record the canonical interpretation expected at validation and connection.

Prove the minimum capability: resolution, connect, HTTP request, redirect, or response read. Internal/cloud destinations are not the default next step; use controlled internal fixtures and policy ASK where relevant. Never scan ranges or ports.
